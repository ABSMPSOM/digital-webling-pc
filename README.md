## 🔮 What is this?
Most digital wellbeing apps just make you feel bad for being on your PC. **DigitalWebling Ultra** gamifies your focus. It tracks your active windows locally, calculates your daily **Aura Score**, and levels up your real-life RPG skills based on what apps you use. 

Grinding LeetCode in VS Code? +100 Aura. Staring at Discord for 4 hours? You're cooked (Aura deficit).

### ⚡ The Sauce (Features)
* 🧠 **Zero System Lag:** Uses an aggressive PID-caching engine. It monitors your apps without polling the OS process tree, meaning it uses practically **0% CPU**. Your Valorant FPS stays untouched.
* 🏆 **Aura & Vibe Check:** Real-time productivity scoring. Ranks you from *Starter* to *Mythic* based on your daily focus ratio.
* ⚔️ **RPG Skill Tree:** Screen time translates directly to stats: `Intelligence` (Coding), `Reflexes` (Gaming), `Creativity` (Design/Music), and `Charisma` (Social).
* 📊 **Deep Analytics:** 30-day rolling skyline charts, category donut charts, and peak day tracking.
* 🔒 **100% Local, Zero Feds:** No cloud syncing. No background telemetry. Data is stored in a local `.json` file and auto-prunes anything older than 30 days.
