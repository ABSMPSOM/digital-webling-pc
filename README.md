## 🔮 What is DigitalWebling Ultra?
Standard digital wellbeing apps are boring. **DigitalWebling Ultra** turns your daily screen time into a game. Whether you are deep in a coding session, listening to Lofi vibes, or grinding competitive matches, this app tracks your active window, calculates your productivity "Vibe Score," and levels up your RPG skill tree—all while running locally with virtually **0% CPU footprint**. 

Engineered specifically so it won't drop your framerates during intense gaming sessions or eat up memory while compiling code.

---

## 🚀 Epic Features

### 📡 Live Focus Dashboard
A highly visual, neon-accented command center showing exactly where your time is going today.
* **Live "Aura" Score:** Tracks your productivity in real-time. Coding (VS Code, PyCharm) boosts your aura; doom-scrolling lowers it.
* **Animated Progress:** Sleek, eased animations for UI elements and progress bars.

### 📊 30-Day Visualization
A beautiful mini focus skyline plotting your daily habits.
* **Trend Charts:** See your daily peak activity over a rolling 30-day window.
* **Category Mix:** Donut charts breaking down your usage by Dev, Gaming, Browsing, and more.

### 🏆 Rankings & Leaderboards
Compete against your past self.
* **App Leaderboard:** See which apps dominate your top lane over the last 1, 7, or 30 days.
* **Focus Tier:** Rank up from *Starter* to *Mythic* based on your productive hours.

### ⚔️ RPG Skill Tree
Your screen time translates directly to RPG stats:
* **Intelligence:** Leveled up by coding (VS Code, CMD, Terminals).
* **Reflexes:** Leveled up by gaming (Valorant, Steam).
* **Creativity:** Leveled up by design and music (Photoshop, Figma, Spotify).
* **Charisma:** Influenced by communication apps (Discord, Teams).

### 🔒 100% Local & Fast
* **30-Day Auto-Pruning:** The app only remembers the last 30 days to keep the local JSON database lightning-fast and lightweight.
* **Zero Cloud:** No data leaves your machine. Ever.
